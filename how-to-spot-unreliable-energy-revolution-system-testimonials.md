## Key checks before you proceed

### Evaluate reviews carefully

Look for specific descriptions of what was received, when it arrived, and what was tested. Give less weight to identical wording, vague superlatives, unverified earnings or savings, and reviews that only repeat sales claims. A balanced review explains limits and assumptions.

### Consider realistic energy savings

Home electricity costs depend on tariff, weather, insulation, appliances, household behavior, and local rules. A guide alone does not demonstrate a reduction in bills. Compare any projected savings with your own usage data and a transparent calculation.

### Check scientific consistency

Energy conservation matters: a device cannot supply continuous useful electrical output without an energy input. Terms such as free energy, resonance, and Tesla-inspired do not remove that requirement. Ask for independently measured input and output over a meaningful period.

### Record evidence before contacting support

Keep the product title, purchase date, invoice number, transaction reference, file type, error message, and a brief timeline. Share only the minimum necessary information through a verified contact channel. Never include a password or full payment card number in a support message.

### Use a measured comparison

Create a simple table for each option with purchase cost, ongoing cost, required skills, expected benefit, proof quality, warranty, and risk. Mark unknown fields as unknown rather than filling them with assumptions. This makes competing offers easier to compare.

### How should I handle an unsatisfactory purchase?

Review the documented refund conditions, contact the verified seller promptly, keep correspondence, and consult your payment provider if a dispute remains unresolved. Avoid sharing sensitive account credentials.
