## Key checks before you proceed

### Separate claims from proof

Write down the exact claim and what measurable result it predicts. Distinguish a theoretical idea from a tested design, and distinguish a personal story from independent measurements. If no reproducible method, equipment list, or credible test is supplied, treat the claim as unverified.

### Understand the format

Check whether the offer is a downloadable document, printed book, video course, consultation, or kit. Confirm whether any tools, components, subscriptions, or outside services are required. Do not infer that a product includes hardware merely because promotional images show electrical equipment.

### Compare the real costs

List the advertised charge, taxes, shipping, payment-processing charges, required tools, consumables, and any optional upgrades. If a total is not published, request it in writing before paying. Compare against ordinary energy-efficiency improvements using the same time horizon and assumptions.

### Check electrical safety

Do not connect homemade generation equipment to household wiring without qualified review, code-compliant protection, and applicable permits. Household electrical projects can create shock, fire, and backfeed hazards. For any proposed installation, consult a licensed electrician and the local utility.

### Protect payment information

Use a payment method with clear dispute protections and keep the order confirmation. Verify the checkout domain and avoid sending passwords, card numbers, or identity documents to an unsolicited contact. Save screenshots of advertised terms before completing an order.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Energy Revolution System book a verified way to reduce my electricity bill?

Not on the basis of a name or promotional description alone. Savings depend on an actual method, independently credible evidence, implementation costs, and household conditions. Verify the specific claims before relying on them.

### How can I find the current price?

Check the exact seller checkout and written offer terms at the time you intend to purchase. Prices and availability can change, and a displayed guide price may exclude implementation costs.

### Does the product include hardware or a printed book?

Do not assume either. Read the inclusions and delivery description and obtain clarification if the format is unclear. Keep the written answer with your purchase records.

### What should I do if I cannot access a purchased download?

Check your order email, account downloads area, spam folder, and device file compatibility. Then contact the seller identified on your receipt with the order date and a description of the issue.

### Can I build electrical equipment from instructions without professional help?

Electrical work may require permits, qualified installation, inspection, and utility approval. Never connect an experimental device to home wiring without professional review and required safety controls.

### How should I handle an unsatisfactory purchase?

Review the documented refund conditions, contact the verified seller promptly, keep correspondence, and consult your payment provider if a dispute remains unresolved. Avoid sharing sensitive account credentials.

## Final checklist

Before deciding, confirm the exact seller, what you receive, the full cost, the evidence behind technical claims, the refund rules, and the safest next step. Keep notes on any unanswered questions. A cautious comparison is more useful than a rushed purchase based on an unverified promise.
