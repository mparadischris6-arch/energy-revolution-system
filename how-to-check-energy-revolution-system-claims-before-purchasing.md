## Key checks before you proceed

### Understand the format

Check whether the offer is a downloadable document, printed book, video course, consultation, or kit. Confirm whether any tools, components, subscriptions, or outside services are required. Do not infer that a product includes hardware merely because promotional images show electrical equipment.

### Compare the real costs

List the advertised charge, taxes, shipping, payment-processing charges, required tools, consumables, and any optional upgrades. If a total is not published, request it in writing before paying. Compare against ordinary energy-efficiency improvements using the same time horizon and assumptions.

### Check electrical safety

Do not connect homemade generation equipment to household wiring without qualified review, code-compliant protection, and applicable permits. Household electrical projects can create shock, fire, and backfeed hazards. For any proposed installation, consult a licensed electrician and the local utility.

### Protect payment information

Use a payment method with clear dispute protections and keep the order confirmation. Verify the checkout domain and avoid sending passwords, card numbers, or identity documents to an unsolicited contact. Save screenshots of advertised terms before completing an order.

### Read the refund terms

Find the written refund window, exclusions, eligible formats, cancellation process, and support channel. Distinguish a seller guarantee from a platform payment policy. Keep dated copies of your request and correspondence in case you need to follow up.
